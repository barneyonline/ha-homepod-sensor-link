# Climate Pod — Apple-inspired colour variants

Three alternatives based on the original Climate Pod concept. These are original generated concepts inspired by Apple colour families, not official Apple assets or exact brand colour specifications. HomePod Midnight was subsequently selected; refined distribution assets now live in `custom_components/homepod_sensor_link/brand/`. These original concept files remain unchanged.

| Variant | Palette | Transparent PNG |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apple Home Warm | Golden yellow, orange and ivory | [01-apple-home.png](01-apple-home.png) |
| HomePod Blue | Deep blue, icy blue, orange and ivory | [02-homepod-blue.png](02-homepod-blue.png) |
| HomePod Midnight | Midnight, graphite and silver with a cyan/violet/pink top | [03-homepod-midnight.png](03-homepod-midnight.png) |

All PNGs are 1254 × 1254 with alpha transparency. Original concepts and integration branding are unchanged. Built-in image generation edited the original Climate Pod reference; the resulting PNGs were copied without pixel modification. Full prompts are in [PROMPTS.md](PROMPTS.md).

Open [index.html](index.html) for the same images on white and charcoal surfaces at 144, 48 and 32 CSS pixels. [light-dark-preview.jpg](light-dark-preview.jpg) records the browser comparison. All three retain visible silhouettes and sensor symbols on both surfaces; fine details soften at 32 pixels. HomePod Blue offers clear temperature/humidity colour separation. Midnight gives the strongest neutral treatment with a small colour accent.

These remain design concepts. Inspect and clean the slightly rough alpha edges, refine optical sizing and prepare the distribution formats before adopting a production mark. No integration, Home Assistant installation or public branding service was changed.

Colour inspiration: [Apple Home design guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/homekit), [Apple HomePod model colours](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101609), and [Apple's HomePod mini midnight announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2024/07/apple-introduces-homepod-mini-in-midnight/). No official hexadecimal colour values are asserted.
